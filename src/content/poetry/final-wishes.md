englishStanzas:
  - |-
    May the one reciting the Party Constitution in the corridor of Pinyuan One before dawn,
    may he never forget a word he has read,
    and never be tortured by his conscience
    May those who firmly believe in freedom democracy civility harmony
    may they succeed, and may freedom be freedom,
    democracy be democracy, may all whose hearts are higher than heaven,
    escape the curse of a fate thinner than paper
  - |-
    May the idealists among us,
    may their fixations be tolerated, may the place one foot
    higher than reality have better air quality
    May the ostriches in the crowd, may the necks they plunge
    into the soil never wake with a crick
    May those who, like me, are nothing,
    understand nothing, can do nothing about anything
    may all such ordinary people
    have the right to remain ordinary
  - |-
    May we never meet again on a battlefield,
    in a cowshed or a concentration camp
    May we never have to throw ourselves into a lake,
    never have to plead guilty with broken, bleeding heads
    May we have life, a hometown, and family,
    and if not all three, then two of them at least
chineseStanzas:
  - |-
    祝愿在凌晨品一的楼道背诵党章的人，
    祝他过目不忘，
    还能永不受良心的折磨
    祝愿笃信自由民主文明和谐的人
    祝他们成功，也祝愿自由是自由，
    民主是民主，所有心比天高的人，
    逃脱命比纸薄的诅咒
  - |-
    祝愿我们中的理想主义者
    祝他们的偏执被包容，祝比现实
    高一尺的地方，空气质量更好
    祝愿人群中的鸵鸟，祝他们插在
    土里的脖子永不落枕
    祝愿像我一样，什么都不是，
    什么都不懂，什么都无可作为的人
    祝愿所有这样的普通人
    拥有保持普通的权利
  - |-
    祝愿我们不在战场、
    牛棚和集中营里重逢
    祝愿我们不必投湖，
    不必头破血流着请罪
    祝愿我们拥有生命，故乡和亲人，
    哪怕不是都有，也有其中两个
